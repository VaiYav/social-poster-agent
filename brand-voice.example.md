# Brand Voice

Copy this file to `brand-voice.md` and replace the bracketed placeholders with your project details.

## Brand Identity

- **Brand name:** [Your Brand]
- **Domain / topic area:** [e.g. AI tooling, climate tech, personal finance]
- **One-line description:** [What you help people do, in plain language]
- **Audience:** [Who you are writing for — role, experience level, interests]

## Voice

- Direct, practical, and human.
- Avoid corporate jargon and AI-sounding phrases.
- Write like a knowledgeable peer, not a salesperson.
- Use short sentences and line breaks that work for mobile feeds.

## Tone

- **On social posts:** confident, helpful, occasionally wry.
- **On replies/comments:** warm, curious, respectful.
- **On technical topics:** precise but not pedantic.

## Content Principles

- Lead with the insight, not the setup.
- Use concrete examples and specific numbers when possible.
- Avoid generic claims like "we're revolutionizing" or "game-changing."
- No made-up statistics or unverifiable facts.

## Example Posts

### Example 1 — Short take
> The best automation is the one your team actually trusts. Start with one boring, repeated task and make it invisible.

### Example 2 — Thread hook
> I used to ship features without checking whether anyone used them. Three habits changed that:
>
> 1. Instrument every new flow before launch.
> 2. Pick one metric you will actually act on.
> 3. Review it weekly, not quarterly.

## Anti-Patterns

- Avoid: "In today's fast-paced world..."
- Avoid: "delve into", "landscape", "synergy", "leverage"
- Avoid: asking for personal data or private details in replies.
- Avoid: self-promotional links dropped into unrelated conversations.
